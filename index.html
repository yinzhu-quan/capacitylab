Parameters

Symbols used by the model

Daily factors — Beta on [0, 1]
RReliability that day — fraction of time the resource actually runs (0–1); mean written r
QQuality that day — fraction of output that is good (0–1); mean written q
EEfficiency that day — actual speed as a fraction of rated speed (0–1); mean written e
Derived capacity
Gross capCapacity if fully utilized, units per day
Net capCapacity after reliability, quality, efficiency losses
Model formulas▶
Derived capacity (per working day)
Gross cap= N × a ÷ t
r·q·e= mean(R) × mean(Q) × mean(E)
Est net cap= (r·q·e) × Gross cap
Daily calculation
Net capacity= w × ⌊ a × N × (R × Q × E) ÷ t ⌋
Production= min( Net cap, d + max(0, Target − Init) )
Sales= max( 0, min( d, Production + Init ) )
Lost sales= max( 0, d − Sales )
Final stock= max( 0, Init + Production − Sales )
Init= Target (day 1); prev Final stock (day > 1)
Ratios & summary
Service level= Sales ÷ d  (= 1 if d = 0)
Gross Capacity Utilization= Production ÷ Gross cap
Net Capacity Utilization= Production ÷ Net cap  (0 on off-days)
